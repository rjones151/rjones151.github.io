**Hello, I’m Rhiannon. Welcome to my website.**

I created it to host my thoughts on technology, inclusion, productivity, writing, and design. You can also expect the occasional map.

Professionally, I work at the intersection of data and product, currently building tech products for marketers.

In my free time, I like to read. You can find a few books that have shaped me on my [bookshelf](/bookshelf).

I also write. Mostly for myself, but also for others. From 2020 - 2022 I grew a lifestyle blog from 0 to 35k+ monthly readers, but shut it down to focus on other projects. I miss it though, and the community was unmatched, which is why I created this website.

I can’t promise a set theme or schedule, but you can expect thoughts on product, careers, governance, inclusion, and anything else worth sharing. If that seems interesting, feel free to stick around.
