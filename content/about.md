**Hello, I’m Rhiannon. Welcome to my website.**

I created it to host my thoughts on technology, inclusion, productivity, writing, and design. As a data viz nerd, you can also expect the occasional map.

Professionally, I work in the intersection of data and product - currently building tech products for marketers.

In my free time, I like to read. Mostly fiction, but I also enjoy books on business, productivity, and product development. You can find a few that have shaped me on my bookshelf.

I spend a lot of time writing. Mostly for myself, but also for others. From 2020 - 2022 I grew a lifestyle blog from 0 to 35k+ monthly readers, but shut it down to focus on my career.

I miss it though, and the community was unmatched, which is why I created this website.

I can’t promise a set theme or schedule, but you can expect thoughts on product, careers, and anything else worth sharing. If that seems interesting, feel free to stick around.

Growing up I drew a lot. I set it aside as I began to prioritise maths and tech, but I never stopped thinking as an artist.

Recently, I’ve been working through [Baseline’s design course](https://www.baselinehq.com/), building projects with [Affinity products](https://www.affinity.studio/), and I even started a good old fashioned sketchbook.

Designing products has been a full-circle moment: I get to combine my first love of art with my second love of technology.

I play video games (*Balatro, Stardew Valley, Dave the Diver, Inscryption, Civ 5, Witcher 3*), and board games (*chess, mahjong*). Turn-based strategy games are my jam. If you know a few, let me know.
